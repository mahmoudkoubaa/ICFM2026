Submission Guidelines

Language

English is the official language of the conference. All submissions must be written in English.

Format

Abstracts must be submitted as a one-page PDF using the official ICFM 2026 template.

Presentation Type

Authors may choose Oral or Poster presentation. The Scientific Committee reserves the right to reassign based on submissions.

Open Access

Publication Opportunities

Selected high-quality papers presented at ICFM 2026 will be recommended for publication in special issues of indexed international journals (Scopus / Web of Science), subject to rigorous peer review.
